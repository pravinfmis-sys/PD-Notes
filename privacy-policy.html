PD Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App: PD Notes · Package: com.noteflow.app

PD Notes is an offline-first notebook application. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how Android backup works.

1. Information stored on your device

PD Notes stores notebook and folder names, page text and formatting, freehand ink strokes, placed images, signatures, shape stencils, imported PDF page images, and app preferences in the app's private storage.

This content is not sent to a PD Notes server. The app has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or tracking services.

2. Information we collect

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information from PD Notes. PD Notes does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or broad storage.

Images and PDFs are read only after you choose them through Android's system photo picker or document picker. The app copies the selected content into its private storage so the notebook can open it later.

3. Android Auto Backup

PD Notes supports Android Auto Backup. If you enable system backup on your Android device and your device is signed in to a Google account, Android may back up the app database, imported images/PDFs, and preferences to Google's backup service. Google controls that backup service and its encryption; the developer does not receive or have access to the backed-up data.

You can disable or manage Android backup in your device's system settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; restoring from backup depends on the device and Google account settings. Android backup quotas are limited, so very large image- or PDF-heavy notebooks may not fully back up.

4. PDF import and export

  • PDF export: you choose the destination through Android's system create-document picker. The exported PDF is written to the location you select.
  • PDF import: you choose a PDF through Android's system document picker. Each page is rendered into a private image inside the app so it can be placed in the notebook.

PD Notes does not upload imported or exported PDFs to a developer server.

5. Permissions

PD Notes does not request Android runtime permissions. File selection uses the system photo picker and document picker, which grant access only to the item you choose.

6. Children's privacy

PD Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system or developer-operated data collection. Content remains on the device and, if enabled, in the user's Android backup.

7. Data retention and deletion

Notebook content stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. PDF exports remain wherever you saved them until you delete them with a file manager. Android backup retention is controlled by Google and your backup settings.

8. Security

App data is stored in Android's app-private storage. Android backup, when enabled, is managed and encrypted by Google under the user's account settings. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so keep your device lock and Google account protected.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app or the store listing before the change takes effect.
